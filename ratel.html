Civic initiative

Ratel Movement

A civic-wave initiative focused on accountability, mobilisation, and community-led action across Nigeria.

ApproachCampaignsJoin

Approach

Mobilise with purpose

Ratel is built around collective civic energy. It channels citizens into non-violent accountability actions while keeping community voice at the center of every campaign.

🧹

Nationwide sanitation drives

Large-scale cleanups are organised across communities to renew public spaces and promote civic responsibility.

🤝

Online solidarity campaigns

Digital campaigns connect citizens, amplify accountability messages, and create momentum around shared civic goals.

🌱

Grassroots community engagement

Local leaders and volunteers lead actions that reflect the unique needs of neighborhoods and everyday Nigerians.

Campaign

Accountability in action

The Ratel Movement drives campaigns that demand better performance from institutions, while promoting non-violent civic participation.

Campaign

Community solidarity

Online and offline campaigns are designed to strengthen public solidarity, push for transparency, and support vulnerable citizens.

“We are not shutting down the country with violence — but with impact.”

How to join Ratel

Join through civic events, digital campaigns, and volunteer activities. The movement welcomes citizens who want to turn concern into structured civic engagement.

  • Engage in online solidarity campaigns
  • Support grassroots community actions
